Our story

A little piece of home, built here in Texas

I moved to Texas more than three years ago. I built a life here, and I love this state — but there were nights I missed home so much it was hard to put into words: my mom's table, the smell of the grill on a Sunday, a whole family crowded around the fire waiting for the first chorizo to be ready.

El Fogón TX started as a way to fix that for myself, and it grew into something bigger: a place where anyone missing Argentina can feel closer to it, and where anyone visiting Texas can sit down to a real, honest asado, maybe for the first time.

This is a table for sharing — with family, with friends, with people you just met. The fire is always lit, the chimichurri is always homemade, and the door is always open.
